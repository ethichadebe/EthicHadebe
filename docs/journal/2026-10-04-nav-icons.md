# 2026-10-04 — Matching logo and mail icons

- **Asked for:** the logo and the mail icon at the same size, with the same margins and padding.
- **Worked first time:** yes.
- **Laptop needed:** no.

## What was wrong

- The logo was 50px tall and the mail icon 75px, each with its own margins.
- Both were tiny PNGs (47px and 71px) stretched to that size, so they looked soft on a phone's high-density screen. Matching them by stretching the logo further would have made it worse.

## What changed

- Both icons are redrawn as SVG (`src/components/Navbar/NavIcons.jsx`): the logo from its exact 45×46 pixel grid, the mail badge as a green circle with a white ring and envelope. Their colours come from the design tokens, so they stay in step with `--color-accent`.
- Each SVG is cropped tight to its shape, so at the same CSS size they look the same size.
- One size and one margin for both: `--nav-icon-size: 56px` and `--nav-icon-margin: 10px` in `index.css`. The navbar height is now derived from them (56 + 2 × 10 = 76px), so the scroll offsets and the burger menu's drop-down follow automatically.
- The same hover (fade to 80%) on both.
- `Logo.png` and `Mail.png` removed; nothing else used them.

Measured at 360, 390, 768 and 1366px: both icons 56×56, 30px from their edge of the screen, level with each other; burger and desktop links still exactly centred; menu links land at 76px, just below the navbar.
